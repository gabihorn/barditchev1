! ------------------------------------------------------------------
! Manual version of the rules (paste into AdGuard Home:
! Filters -> Custom filtering rules) if you do NOT use the control server.
! Replace 192.168.1.50 with the IP of the Windows PC.
! To unblock: put "!" at the start of the || lines (comments them out).
! ------------------------------------------------------------------

! Block YouTube (all subdomains: www, m, music, studio...) for the PC only
||youtube.com^$client=192.168.1.50
||youtu.be^$client=192.168.1.50
||youtube-nocookie.com^$client=192.168.1.50

! Keep YouTube Kids and Google sign-in working
@@||youtubekids.com^$client=192.168.1.50
@@||accounts.youtube.com^$client=192.168.1.50

! Do NOT block these - YouTube Kids streams video and images from them:
!   googlevideo.com, ytimg.com, ggpht.com, youtubei.googleapis.com
! (That is also why the built-in "Blocked services -> YouTube" must stay OFF:
!  it blocks googlevideo.com and breaks YouTube Kids.)

! Optional: stop browsers from bypassing AdGuard with their own DNS-over-HTTPS
! use-application-dns.net makes Firefox turn DoH off automatically.
||use-application-dns.net^
